# Marketing Measurement Science

An interactive guide to the statistical methods, causal inference frameworks, and analytical models used in marketing measurement and decision-making.

The guide connects measurement planning, outcome modeling, causal effect estimation, attribution, and resource allocation through theoretical explanations, worked examples, interactive visualizations, and statistical calculators.

[Explore Marketing Measurement Science](https://8139causal.github.io/measurement/)

## Topics

- Measurement planning and KPI development
- Statistical inference and hypothesis testing
- Experimental and quasi-experimental methods
- Causal inference and incrementality
- Attribution and calibration
- Media mix modeling
- Customer analytics and market segmentation
- Customer lifetime value, churn, and retention
- Budget allocation and optimization

    Incrementality

    Compare revenue attributed to a campaign with the additional revenue estimated from a randomized control group.

    Apparent Return on Ad Spend

    In this hypothetical example, $1.7 million of observed revenue divided by $100,000 in ad spend gives 17×. The numerator includes revenue that would have occurred without advertising.

    $1,700,000
    Observed Revenue
    $100,000
    Ad Spend
    17×
    Apparent ROAS

    Working with historical records? Use Causal Graphs and Identification to specify a plausible comparison.

    Incremental Return on Ad Spend

    The test group produces $200,000 more revenue than an equally sized randomized control group, giving an incremental revenue ROAS of 2×. At 40% contribution margin, that is $80,000 in incremental contribution before the $100,000 ad cost. An interval needs unit-level revenue variability and the assignment design. See how to report uncertainty for an experiment.

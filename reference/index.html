Glossary

Lift
In a test, the difference relative to a comparison baseline. In a market basket, the observed rate of buying items together divided by the rate expected under independence.
Propensity
In an observational design, the probability of treatment given covariates. A response model can instead estimate the probability of an outcome such as churn or purchase. Uplift compares outcomes under treatment and control.
Calibration
Adjusting one credit estimate using another source of evidence. The holdout exercise uses a test ratio. The survey exercise rescales platform ROAS against survey ROAS.
Return
Total return measures the overall modeled output. Average return relates total output to total spend. Marginal return relates a small change in spending to its additional output. Incremental ROAS uses revenue attributed to the intervention by an effect estimate.

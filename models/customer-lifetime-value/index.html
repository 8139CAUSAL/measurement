    Customer Lifetime Value

    Estimate lifetime sales from historical averages or discount expected subscription gross profit. Use RFM separately to summarize purchase behavior on a 3–15 scale.

    Historical revenue

    Multiply historical averages to estimate lifetime revenue per customer. Accurate average tenure requires complete customer histories or an estimate that accounts for customers still active.

    Lifetime revenue = Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan

    RFM scoring

    Enter recency, frequency, and monetary scores from 1 to 5. The example uses fixed cutoffs for the sum, with higher scores representing more recent or frequent purchases and higher spend.

    RFM Score = R + F + M Thresholds: 3–7 = Low score  |  8–11 = Mid score  |  12–15 = High score
    5 = purchased very recently, 1 = long ago
    5 = purchases very often, 1 = rarely
    5 = highest spender, 1 = lowest

    Discounted subscription CLV

    Assume constant gross profit while active, a full first month, and a constant chance of churn at the end of each month. Discount each month’s gross profit back to the start of the subscription. Acquisition cost is excluded from the calculation.

    CLV = Monthly gross profit ÷ (Monthly churn + monthly discount rate)

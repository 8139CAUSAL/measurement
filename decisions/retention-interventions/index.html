    Retention Interventions

    Estimate how much a retention offer changes churn, then compare the value of the expected additional retention with the cost of sending the offer.

    Separate churn risk from response to an offer

    Historical activity can help predict who will churn. A randomized offer and control group can estimate the average change in retention for eligible customers. Use the same population and time horizon for estimated lift, retained profit, and cost.

    See churn prediction and treatment-effect estimates by profile.

    Retention Worthiness Calculator

    Multiply the change in retention probability by the profit associated with an additional retained customer. Compare it with total expected offer cost per eligible customer.

    Use profit over the same horizon as the churn probabilities.
    Incremental, e.g. 40% → 30% = 10

    Reconcile Credit

    Compare journey rules, platform claims, survey responses, and holdout evidence.

    • Attribution RulesCompare first-touch, last-touch, linear, and U-shaped credit on one journey.
    • Markov AttributionFit a transition matrix from ordered journeys and compare modeled removal effects.
    • Platform Credit OverlapCompare the credit three platforms claim with recorded revenue.
    • Holdout CalibrationApply a holdout ratio and examine when it can transfer to another campaign.
    • Survey AttributionSummarize self-reported discovery, check response bias, and compare with platform credit.
